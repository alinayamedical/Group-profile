1. COMPANY OVERVIEW
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Alinaya Alrafia Medical Company is a Saudi healthcare group
headquartered in Riyadh, established in 2023. We develop,
manage, and grow an integrated portfolio of medical businesses
that collectively serve communities across the Kingdom of Saudi
Arabia.

Operating at the intersection of clinical care, medical
technology, home health, and specialized diagnostics, Alinaya
Alrafia Medical Company brings together five complementary
subsidiaries under one unified strategy — delivering quality
care at every point of need.

Aligned with the transformative goals of Saudi Vision 2030,
we are committed to raising the standard of healthcare delivery,
building national healthcare capacity, and contributing to a
healthier, stronger Kingdom.

Tagline: "Diagnostic Precision. Professional Integrity."

3. GROUP STRUCTURE — OUR SUBSIDIARIES
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Alinaya Alrafia Medical Company manages five specialized
subsidiaries, each addressing a distinct segment of the
Saudi healthcare ecosystem:

──────────────────────────────────────────────────
3.1  AL JOOD MEDICAL
     Ambulance Manufacturing & Emergency Vehicle Conversion
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Al Jood Medical specializes in the manufacture and conversion
of ambulances and emergency response vehicles. Supporting the
Kingdom's emergency healthcare infrastructure, Al Jood Medical
produces purpose-built vehicles built to international safety
and performance standards — contributing directly to Vision
2030's goal of strengthening national emergency response
capacity.

Website: aljoodmedical.com

──────────────────────────────────────────────────
3.2  SATECH
     Saudi Technology & Equipment Co.
     Medical, Scientific & Industrial Equipment
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
SATECH is an import and supply company for advanced equipment
across four specializations:

  • Security & Forensic Equipment
  • Medical Laboratory Equipment
  • Scientific Laboratory Equipment
  • Material Testing, Industrial Equipment & Accessories
  • Specialty Contact Lens

SATECH serves hospitals, diagnostic laboratories, government
agencies, academic institutions, and industrial clients across
the Kingdom, bridging global technology with local demand.

LinkedIn: linkedin.com/company/saudi-technology-&-equipment-co--satech-/

──────────────────────────────────────────────────
3.3  ALWAROOD MEDICAL CENTER
     Multi-Specialty Outpatient Clinical Care
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Alwarood Medical Center is a multi-specialty outpatient clinic
offering comprehensive primary and specialist care to patients.
With a patient-centered approach and qualified medical team,
Alwarood is a trusted destination for families seeking quality,
accessible healthcare in the community.

Website:   alwarood.com
Instagram: @alwaroodmedical

──────────────────────────────────────────────────
3.4  ALKHAWALED DIALYSIS CENTER
     Specialized Kidney & Renal Care
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Alkhawaled is a dedicated kidney dialysis center providing
life-sustaining renal care to patients in need. Operating
with trained nephrology support staff and modern dialysis
equipment, Alkhawaled is committed to patient comfort,
clinical safety, and consistent treatment quality.

Facebook: facebook.com/Alkhawaled1/

──────────────────────────────────────────────────
3.5  MEDOOR
     Home Health Care Services
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Medoor delivers professional healthcare services directly to
patients in their homes — bringing clinical-grade care to
those who need it most, where they are most comfortable.

Services include:
  • Home nursing and post-operative care
  • Home dental care
  • Physiotherapy and rehabilitation
  • Chronic disease management at home
  • Elder care and long-term home health support

Website: medoor.com.sa (medoor.sa — pending trademark registration)

4. OUR COMMITMENT TO SAUDI VISION 2030
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Saudi Vision 2030 sets out a bold agenda for transforming the
Kingdom's healthcare sector — increasing private sector
participation, improving quality standards, strengthening
emergency infrastructure, and expanding access to care for all.

Alinaya Alrafia Medical Company is an active participant in
this transformation:

  • Al Jood Medical supports the localization of emergency
    vehicle manufacturing in Saudi Arabia.

  • SATECH supplies locally-distributed advanced equipment,
    reducing dependence on direct international procurement.

  • Alwarood Medical Center and Alkhawaled Dialysis Center
    expand accessible clinical and specialized care for
    Saudi communities.

  • Medoor addresses the growing demand for home-based care —
    a key pillar of modern, patient-centered healthcare models.

We are proud to contribute to the Kingdom's healthcare future
and to stand alongside the national leadership in building
a healthier Saudi Arabia.
