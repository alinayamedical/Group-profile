Group Overview

A Diversified Holding Platform Built for Saudi Arabia

TAG Holding Group is a Saudi holding company based in Riyadh, led by Founder and Chairman Dr. Abdulkader Al Gilani. With over 30 years of operational and entrepreneurial experience in healthcare and business development, the Group has built a diversified portfolio of operating companies and development-stage ventures.

The Group's activities span healthcare services, wellness and beauty, events management, and professional development — with all businesses aligned to the opportunities presented by Saudi Vision 2030 and the Kingdom's expanding private sector.
