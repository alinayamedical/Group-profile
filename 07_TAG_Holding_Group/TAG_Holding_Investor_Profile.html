Group Overview

A Diversified Holding Platform Built for Saudi Arabia

TAG Holding Group is a Saudi holding company based in Riyadh, with a diversified portfolio of operating companies and development-stage ventures founded on a legacy of healthcare and entrepreneurship.

The Group's activities span healthcare services, dialysis, medical and scientific services, wellness and beauty, events management, professional development, and technology — with all businesses aligned to the opportunities presented by Saudi Vision 2030 and the Kingdom's expanding private sector.
