Group Overview

A Diversified Holding Platform Built for Saudi Arabia

TAG Holding Group is a Saudi holding company based in Riyadh, with a diversified portfolio of businesses founded on a legacy of healthcare and entrepreneurship.

The Group's activities span healthcare services, ambulance manufacturing, medical equipment supply, dialysis, medical and scientific services, wellness and beauty, events management, hospitality training, and professional development — with all businesses aligned to the opportunities presented by Saudi Vision 2030 and the Kingdom's expanding private sector.
