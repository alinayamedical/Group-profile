About TAG Holding

A Diversified Holding Platform Built for Saudi Arabia

TAG Holding Group is a Saudi holding company based in Riyadh, with a diversified portfolio of businesses founded on a legacy of healthcare and entrepreneurship.

The Group's activities span healthcare services, ambulance manufacturing, medical equipment supply, dialysis, medical and scientific services, wellness and beauty, events management, hospitality training, and professional development — aligned to the opportunities of Saudi Vision 2030 and the Kingdom's expanding private sector.

Anchored in values of excellence, trust, and innovation, TAG Holding works to deliver meaningful outcomes across every sector it serves.

Our Subsidiaries

Group Structure — Our Subsidiaries

TAG brings together specialized businesses addressing distinct segments of the Saudi healthcare ecosystem.

Al Jood Medical

Ambulance Manufacturing & Emergency Vehicle Conversion

Al Jood Medical specializes in the manufacture and conversion of ambulances and emergency response vehicles, supporting the Kingdom's emergency healthcare infrastructure through purpose-built vehicle solutions.

SATECH

Saudi Technology & Equipment Co. — Medical, Scientific & Industrial Equipment

SATECH imports and supplies specialized equipment and products for healthcare, scientific, security, and industrial applications across the Kingdom.

  • Security & Forensic Equipment
  • Medical Laboratory Equipment
  • Scientific Laboratory Equipment
  • Material Testing, Industrial Equipment & Accessories
  • Specialty Contact Lenses

SATECH serves hospitals, diagnostic laboratories, government agencies, academic institutions, and industrial clients.

Alwarood Medical Center

Multi-Specialty Outpatient Clinical Care

Alwarood Medical Center is a multi-specialty outpatient clinic providing primary and specialist care with a patient-centered approach to community healthcare.
