About TAG Holding

A Diversified Holding Platform Built for Saudi Arabia

TAG Holding Group is a Saudi holding company based in Riyadh, built on a legacy of healthcare and entrepreneurship. The Group's activities span healthcare services, ambulance manufacturing, medical equipment supply, dialysis, wellness and beauty, events management, and professional development.

Anchored in values of excellence, trust, and innovation, TAG Holding aligns its portfolio with the opportunities of Saudi Vision 2030 and the Kingdom's expanding private sector.

Healthcare Businesses

Alinaya Medical — Healthcare Businesses

Healthcare Businesses Managed by Alinaya

The following businesses operate under the management of Alinaya Alrafia Medical Company, TAG Holding's dedicated healthcare subsidiary.

Al Jood Medical

Ambulance Manufacturing & Emergency Vehicle Conversion

Al Jood Medical specializes in the manufacture and conversion of ambulances and emergency response vehicles, supporting the Kingdom's emergency healthcare infrastructure through purpose-built vehicle solutions.

SATECH

Saudi Technology & Equipment Co. — Medical, Scientific & Industrial Equipment

SATECH imports and supplies specialized equipment and products for healthcare, scientific, security, and industrial applications across the Kingdom.

  • Security, forensic & surveillance equipment
  • Medical, scientific & laboratory equipment
  • Material testing, industrial & specialty products

SATECH serves hospitals, diagnostic laboratories, government agencies, academic institutions, and industrial clients.

Alwarood Medical Center

Multi-Specialty Outpatient Clinical Care

Alwarood Medical Center is a multi-specialty outpatient clinic providing primary and specialist care with a patient-centered approach to community healthcare.

Healthcare Businesses (cont.)

Alinaya Medical — Healthcare Businesses

Alkhawaled, Almajorh & MEDOOR

Alkhawaled Dialysis Center

Specialized Kidney & Renal Care

Alkhawaled Dialysis Center focuses on kidney dialysis and ongoing renal care, with an emphasis on patient comfort, clinical safety, and continuity of treatment.

Almajorh

Medical & Scientific Services · Hospital Development · Medical Supplies

Almajorh provides medical and scientific services with activities spanning hospital and medical center development and medical supplies distribution. The company supports the Kingdom's growing healthcare infrastructure in alignment with Vision 2030 targets.

MEDOOR

Professional Home Healthcare Services

MEDOOR delivers professional clinical services to patients at home across Saudi Arabia, bringing quality care directly to patients where they are most comfortable.

  • Primary home care — GP visits, chronic disease management, post-surgical recovery
  • Specialist care — physiotherapy and psychological support
  • Diagnostic services — blood tests, ECG monitoring, health screenings
