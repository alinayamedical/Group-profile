# Figma Make Prompt — Alinaya Medical Corporate Profile

Design a premium investor-grade corporate profile for **Alinaya Alrafia Medical Company** (شركة العناية الرفيعة الطبية), a Saudi healthcare company founded in Riyadh in 2023, aligned with Vision 2030. The profile must feel like a publication from PwC, Deloitte, or Siemens Healthineers — not a marketing brochure.

---

## Brand

**Tagline EN:** Diagnostic Precision. Professional Integrity.
**Tagline AR:** دقة التشخيص وأمانة المهنة.
**Website:** alinayamedical.com | **Email:** info@alinayamedical.com
**Location:** Riyadh, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia

---

## Colors (Strict — Do Not Change)

- Primary Emerald: `#1B4D3E` — covers, headers, footers
- Gold Accent: `#C9A84C` — dividers, icons, rules (max 15% of design)
- White: `#FFFFFF` — page canvas, text on dark
- Off-White: `#F8F9FC` — alternating section backgrounds
- Charcoal: `#1A1A2E` — body text
- Grey: `#6B7280` — captions, labels
- Border: `#E5E7EB` — card borders

Do not use navy blue, teal, or any blue tones.

---

## Typography (Strict — Do Not Change)

- English: **Raleway** — 400, 600, 700
- Arabic: **Cairo** — 400, 600, 700
- H1: 700 / 60px EN, 52px AR
- H2: 600 / 38px EN, 34px AR
- Body: 400 / 16px / line-height 1.7 EN, 1.8 AR

---

## Logo

Abstract wedge mark: two emerald triangular wedges forming an "A" peak, gold diamond below, white vertical center line. Use white wordmark on Emerald backgrounds. Use full-color wordmark on white/off-white.

---

## Format

A4 portrait, 20mm margins, 12-column grid. 7 pages total.

---

## Pages

**Page 1 — Cover**
Full Emerald background. Centered: white wordmark logo → thin gold rule → "Corporate Profile" (white, Raleway Bold 40px) → "شركة العناية الرفيعة الطبية" (white, Cairo 28px) → thin gold rule → "Riyadh · Kingdom of Saudi Arabia · 2026" (gold, 13px). Subtle faint angular geometric pattern at ~5% opacity in background. No photography.

**Page 2 — Table of Contents**
White background. Numbered list: 1. Company Overview 2. Vision & Mission 3. Core Values 4. Business Activities 5. Strategic Direction 6. Key Figures 7. Contact. Section numbers in gold, titles in charcoal, page numbers right-aligned in grey. Thin gold rules top and bottom.

**Page 3 — Company Overview**
Off-white background. Emerald header bar (full width) with section number "01" in gold and heading "Company Overview / نبذة عن الشركة". Two-column body: English left (LTR), Arabic right (RTL), separated by 1px gold vertical rule. Body text: "Alinaya Alrafia Medical Company is a Saudi healthcare company established in Riyadh in 2023, building a portfolio of integrated medical services across the Kingdom, committed to excellence, trust, and Vision 2030."

**Page 4 — Vision & Mission**
White background. Two equal cards side by side with 1px border (#E5E7EB). Each card: monoline gold outline icon → label in grey small caps → English text → Arabic text (RTL).
- Vision: "To be the most trusted healthcare group in Saudi Arabia, recognized for excellence, compassion, and lasting impact."
- Mission: "To deliver comprehensive, integrated healthcare services that improve lives and raise the standard of medical care across Saudi Arabia."

**Page 5 — Core Values**
Off-white background. Large section heading in Emerald, thin gold rule below. Single body paragraph in two columns (EN left, AR right): "Alinaya is guided by five core values: Excellence — holding the highest standards in every service and interaction; Trust — building lasting relationships through transparency and integrity; Compassion — placing human well-being at the heart of everything we do; Innovation — continuously seeking better approaches to healthcare delivery; and Vision 2030 Alignment — actively contributing to Saudi Arabia's healthcare transformation and national development goals." Arabic column (RTL): "تنبثق أعمال شركة العناية الرفيعة الطبية من خمس قيم جوهرية: التميز في كل خدمة وتفاعل، والثقة المبنية على الشفافية والنزاهة، والإنسانية التي تضع رفاهية الإنسان في صميم عملنا، والابتكار في تقديم الرعاية الصحية، والتوافق مع رؤية 2030 في تحقيق أهداف التنمية الوطنية."

**Page 6 — Our Experience**
Off-white background. Emerald header bar with section heading "Our Experience / خبرتنا". Single body paragraph in two columns (EN left, AR right): "Since our founding in Riyadh in 2023, Alinaya Alrafia Medical Company has built deep expertise across the Saudi healthcare sector. **Page 7 — Contact**
White top 75%, Emerald bottom band 25%. Top: full-color logo centered → thin gold rule → two-column contact details (EN left, AR right): website, email, phone (TBD), address, working hours Sun–Thu 9AM–6PM. Emerald band: tagline in white Raleway Bold centered, Arabic tagline in Cairo Bold below.

## Global Rules

- Icons: monoline outline only, 1.5–2px stroke, gold on light, white on dark
- Dividers: 1px gold horizontal rules between sections
- No gradients, no drop shadows, no rounded corners on layout elements
- Footer on all interior pages: small icon left · company name center (grey, 11px) · page number right (gold)
- Arabic text always RTL, Cairo font, right-aligned
- Avoid: medical crosses, stethoscopes, blue waves, smiling stock doctors, clip art
